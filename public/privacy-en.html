Apollo LLC (hereinafter "the Company") sets forth this Privacy Policy regarding the handling of personal information collected from users of the logical-thinking learning service "Logic" (hereinafter "the Service").

Article 1. Information We Collect

The Service may collect the following information.

Type of informationDetails
Account informationEmail address, display name (optional)
Learning dataLesson completion records, quiz answer history, Fermi estimation responses, roleplay conversation content, learning streaks, XP (experience points)
Billing informationSubscription plan, billing status (payment details such as card numbers are managed by Google Play; the Company does not retain them)
Usage dataAccess timestamps, features used, error logs
Device informationOS version, device type (for crash analysis and product improvement)

Article 2. Purposes of Use

The information we collect is used for the following purposes.

  • Providing, operating, and improving the Service
  • User authentication and account management
  • Recording, analyzing, and personalizing learning progress
  • Generating AI-powered feedback (Fermi estimation, roleplay)
  • Managing subscriptions and processing payments
  • Providing support and responding to inquiries
  • Detecting and preventing fraudulent use
  • Sending Service-related notifications (where the user has opted in)

Article 3. Disclosure to Third Parties

The Company will not provide or disclose user personal information to third parties except in the following cases.

  • Where the user has given consent
  • Where disclosure is required by law
  • Where necessary to protect the life, body, or property of a person
  • To the service providers listed below, only within the scope necessary to provide the Service

In accordance with Article 28 of the Act on the Protection of Personal Information of Japan, we disclose the following information regarding the provision of personal data to third parties located in foreign countries.

  • Country in which recipients are located: All are in the United States of America
  • Personal information protection systems in that country: The United States does not have a comprehensive federal law governing the protection of personal information, but state-level consumer privacy laws (such as the California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act, CCPA/CPRA) and sector-specific federal laws are in place. For details, please refer to the survey published by Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission, "Survey on Personal Information Protection Systems in Foreign Countries" (https://www.ppc.go.jp/personalinfo/legal/kaiseihogohou/)

The Company enters into appropriate agreements (DPAs and similar) with these providers and requires them to handle personal information in accordance with this Privacy Policy
  • Use of input data for AI model training: User input transmitted to Anthropic, PBC is not used for training AI models, in accordance with the standard terms of the Anthropic API

Article 4. Data Storage and Security

  • Learning data and account information are stored on Supabase servers (United States)
  • Application servers are hosted on Render (United States)
  • All communications are encrypted via HTTPS (TLS 1.2 or higher)
  • Passwords are stored in hashed form and never retained in plain text
  • Payment card details do not pass through the Company's servers and are processed directly by Google Play

Article 5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Account information and learning data are retained for as long as the account exists
  • To request account deletion, please contact support@logic-m.com
  • Personally identifiable data will be erased within 30 days of a deletion request (except where retention is required by law)
  • Data stored in device local storage (localStorage) is removed when the app is uninstalled or when browser data is cleared

Article 6. Use of Cookies and Local Storage

The Service uses cookies and local storage for the following purposes.

  • Maintaining login sessions
  • Storing learning progress and settings on the device
  • Saving language preferences

We do not use cookies or tracking for advertising purposes.

Article 7. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 13. If we discover that a user under 13 has registered, we will promptly delete the account.

Article 8. User Rights

Users have the following rights regarding their personal information.

  • Right of access: The right to confirm the personal information we hold about you
  • Right to correction and deletion: The right to request correction of inaccurate information or deletion of your data
  • Right to suspension of use: The right to request that we suspend the use of your personal information

To exercise these rights, please contact support@logic-m.com.
