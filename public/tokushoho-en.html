Service Description

Service nameLogic
Service overviewA training service that provides content covering logical thinking, bookkeeping, project management, and similar subjects, along with AI-powered roleplay, problem generation, Fermi-estimation practice, and a deviation-score ranking feature. Lesson text, explanations, worked examples, quizzes, and other content are generated or edited in whole or in part by AI (Anthropic's Claude API). Because of the nature of AI-generated content, the material may contain errors or inaccuracies.
