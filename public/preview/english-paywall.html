What is MECE?

MECE (pronounced "mee-see") stands for "Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive." It is the most fundamental framework for analyzing and organizing information in business.

■ Mutually Exclusive

No overlap between categories. The same item never appears in more than one bucket.

■ Collectively Exhaustive

Everything is covered. No gaps, no missing pieces.

Why MECE matters:

① Prevents blind spots → better decisions
② Eliminates duplication → efficient resource allocation
③ Clearer communication → team alignment
