Account Deletion Request

Last updated: May 11, 2026 / Applies to: Logic (Mobile App, Android)

Apollo LLC (hereinafter "the Company") provides the following procedure for users of the logical-thinking learning service "Logic" (hereinafter "the Service") who wish to delete their account and associated data.

Article 1. How to Submit a Deletion Request

To request account deletion, please contact us at the email address below. Our support team will review your request, verify your identity, and proceed with the deletion.

Send an email to support@logic-m.com
Please include the following in your email:
  • Registered email address (to identify your account)
  • Scope of deletion (entire account / learning data only / other)
  • Reason for deletion (optional)

Article 2. Data That Will Be Deleted

Upon receiving a deletion request, the following data will be removed.

Type of dataData deleted
Account informationEmail address, display name, Google account linkage
Learning dataLesson completion records, quiz answer history, Fermi estimation responses, roleplay conversation content, learning streaks, XP (experience points)
Subscription informationPlan type, billing status (payment details are not retained by the Company)
Deviation-score rankingPlacement test results and ranking data
Notes and favoritesPersonal memos and bookmarks saved within the app

Article 3. Time Required for Deletion

  • Personally identifiable data will be erased from our servers within 30 days of receiving the deletion request
  • Backup data is fully deleted within 90 days at the latest, in accordance with our regular backup cycle
  • After deletion is complete, we will send a confirmation email to your registered email address

Article 4. Data That Will Not Be Deleted

The following data may be retained for a certain period either because retention is required by law or because the Company has a legitimate business reason to retain it.

  • Accounting books and transaction records (up to 7 years, in accordance with the Corporation Tax Act, the Income Tax Act, and similar laws)
  • Support records (up to 3 years, in anonymized form, retained in preparation for any legal dispute)
  • Fraud-detection logs (up to 1 year, after being converted to a non-personally-identifiable form)
  • Anonymized statistical and analytical data (not subject to deletion, as it cannot identify individuals)
