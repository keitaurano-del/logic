About data deletion and account closure

Warning: deleting your account permanently removes all learning data, progress, and purchase history. This action cannot be undone.

Deletion Steps

1
Tap the button below to open your mail app
2
Check the subject and body, then send the email from the address registered on your account
3
Once we have verified your identity, your account and data will be deleted within 30 days
4
A confirmation email will be sent once deletion is complete
Send deletion request email

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Account information (email address, display name)
  • Learning progress and lesson completion records
  • Fermi estimation answer history
  • XP, level, and streak
  • Subscription information

* Information subject to a statutory retention obligation (such as payment records) may be retained for a certain period.
